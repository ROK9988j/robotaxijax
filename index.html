Robotaxi & Cybercab
Lead · Sep 3

Cybercab hits Austin streets with no steering wheel and no pedals

Tesla launched its purpose-built two-seat Cybercab in Austin, and clips shared on X show the cars moving through public streets with no one behind the wheel. The debut came at an invite-only event that surprised fans expecting a public rollout. Pricing and public-ride availability were held back for a follow-up event. Independent ride data is not yet out, so range and safety numbers remain Tesla's own.

source: Motor1 Quartz Forbes
Expansion · Sep 4

Five metros named next: Las Vegas, Phoenix, Dallas, Houston, Miami

Tesla says months of behind-the-scenes permitting work is underway to launch in the five new regions. No firm launch dates were given. Current driverless-passenger service remains Austin and the San Francisco Bay Area.

source: Drive Tesla
Operations · Sep 4

Austin set to drop onboard safety operators; fleet targeted near 1,500

Tesla plans to move Austin to fully driverless service with no onboard operators before year-end, though the removal may be limited to parts of the geofence at first. Musk put the end-of-year fleet at roughly 1,500 vehicles.

Target and fleet figure are company statements, not confirmed deliveries.
source: Drive Tesla
Tesla, broadly
Analysis · Sep 3

Tesla still trails Waymo, and robotaxi economics are unproven

Coverage of the launch stressed that Tesla remains behind Waymo on driverless miles and paid public rides, in a business no operator has yet shown to be profitable. Treat the launch as a capability milestone, not a revenue one.

source: CNN Business

Autonomous vehicles · Sep 1

Waymo goes live in San Diego, Tampa and Denver; Zoox starts Houston and San Diego testing

Waymo opened driverless service in three more metros, including its first in Colorado. Amazon's Zoox began on-road testing in Houston and San Diego with human safety drivers aboard.

source: Tech Startups CNBC
